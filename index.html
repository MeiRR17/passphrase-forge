72bits
High

Six independent word pairs from 4,096 combinations each produce about 72 bits of random-choice entropy.

How to use this safely

  • Use a different passphrase for every important account.
  • Store it in a trusted password manager, especially if you cannot memorize it.
  • Avoid sharing, screenshots, or saving secrets in documents.
  • Strength depends on how the passphrase is generated, not on decorative characters.
◇

No guessing disguised as science

Entropy is calculated from the size of the generator’s word-pair choices, not from how complicated a phrase appears.
